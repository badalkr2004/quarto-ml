Lecture 8 — Self-check answers

  1. Given i.i.d. samples \(\mathcal{D}\) from unknown \(P\), estimate \(P\) (or conditionals/marginals/moments) and possibly learn to sample.
  2. The marginal \(P_{Y}\) (or \(P(Y=1)\) for prevalence).
  3. The conditional \(P(Y\mid X)\) evaluated at the new image.
  4. Masked pixels can play the role of \(Y\) while visible pixels play \(X\) — still estimating a conditional, with “labels” carved from the data.
  5. Estimate expectations / moments of \(P\) instead of recovering the entire law.
